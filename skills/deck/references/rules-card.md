# Rules card (read this, not the full references, unless your role says otherwise)

Full sources: `pyramid.md`, `logical-writing.md`, `consulting-practices.md`, `source-quality.md`, `layouts.md`, `charts.md`.

## Slide titles
- One affirmative sentence, one action verb, one idea, the whole message of the slide. Two lines at most when possible.
- No question, semicolon, aside, parenthesis, subtitle or SCQA label. Ternary rhythm allowed when the three items share one kind.
- A conclusion, never a topic: "Three regulations force a decision before June", not "Regulatory context".
- Certainty matches the evidence: calibrate with scope ("in the five markets studied"), never with hedging adverbs.
- Titles read alone, in order, tell the story. No idea returns from one slide to another.

## Pyramid, at every level
- An idea summarises the ideas grouped below it; ideas in a group share one kind and follow one order (time, structure, degree, deduction).
- Each block answers a question the slide title raises; the body covers every element of the title and nothing else.
- Block, chart and paragraph titles state the idea their content details.
- SCQA orders the story silently; the executive summary comes last and opens the deck.

## Sentences
- Active voice. One idea per sentence. Known before new; main message before nuance.
- No hedging adverbs (only, now, already, more than, just, very, still, currently).
- No "not X but Y" or "rather than". No em or en dash. No rhetorical scaffolding.
- Parallel lists; one term per concept across the deck.
- Negation test: if the opposite sentence is absurd, the sentence says nothing.

## Data
- Every figure carries unit, scope, date and a named origin. Cite the origin, never the relay.
- No ratio, price or total rebuilt and presented as data. Values computed by the team are marked `calc` and print in blue.
- Source tiers: 1 = producer of the data (regulator, statistics office, filing, study authors); 2 = reputable relay with a method; 3 = blogs, vendor marketing, aggregators (never on a slide).
- Missing data stays visible as "n.d.", never estimated.

## Slides
- White background, grey boxes, one accent for the element the title points to.
- Argument slides: 2 to 4 blocks, 2 to 6 points per block, 25 words at most per point.
- Benchmark grids (`band_matrix`, `table`) may hold many rows; keep one grid per slide and move nothing to an appendix only because it is long.
- Pick the layout from the logical relation between blocks (`layouts.md` table); record relation, alternative and reason in `layout_rationale`.
