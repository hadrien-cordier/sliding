# The reasoning loop: hypothesis-driven, evidence-led, iterative

This reference codifies the method for how to build the substance of a deck.

## Neither thesis-first nor research-first
- **Thesis first, then proof** turns agents into a machine that confirms an initial intuition.
- **Research first, then conclusion** produces an encyclopedia without direction.
- **The loop**: start with a provisional scaffold (an understanding of the problem, frameworks, several possible narratives, structuring questions, intuitions about which analyses discriminate), then let research do three things at once:
  1. **Test existing ideas**: data confirm, qualify or invalidate the first hypotheses.
  2. **Surface new ideas**: some conclusions cannot be imagined before a signal appears in the data.
  3. **Transform the problem**: the initial question may prove ill-posed. A study on the attractiveness of a KYC platform may reveal that the real split runs between banking models, mutualisation levels or regulatory accountability, not between countries.

The scaffold is provisional. Nothing in it is a conclusion to defend.

## One agent owns the convergence, in one context
Separate agents find, check, design and review. That separation protects traceability and prevents self-grading, but it leaves no one owning the intellectual evolution of the problem, and every hand-off forces a cold reread. The engagement manager owns it in a single persistent context: it builds the map, sends researchers out, reads their answers, revises the hypotheses, and writes the storyline without ever handing the reasoning to another agent.

## The reasoning map
The engagement manager keeps one living map, updated in place after each cycle:
1. **Decision**: the decision to support, the reader, the consequences of the decision.
2. **Problem dimensions**: frameworks used as a coverage checklist, never as a ready-made answer (market attractiveness, customer segments, pain points, regulatory feasibility, competition, delivery feasibility, economics...). Every material dimension gets a minimum level of coverage, even when it does not yet support the leading narrative.
3. **Competing narratives**: several possible readings, kept open until research can separate them.
4. **Evidence register**: for each finding, its effect on each narrative, its confidence, its fact-check status and the follow-up question it raises. This is the link between "data found" and "slide produced".
5. **Tensions register**: what resists the story, its implication for what the deck may claim, possible interpretations, and the research that would resolve it.
6. **Open questions**, each with why it matters, current evidence, gap, stop condition and decision impact.

## Two kinds of research
- **Coverage research** (first cycle): look at every material dimension of the problem at a minimum level, to avoid blind spots and surface unexpected angles. Do not deepen any lead prematurely.
- **Discriminant research** (later cycles): separate the narratives still plausible, resolve material contradictions, find missing proof, test extrapolations, and actively search for evidence against the leading narrative.

The system researches neither everything nor only what confirms the favourite hypothesis: it covers the problem enough to avoid blind spots, then concentrates effort on what can change the conclusion.

## Stop rules, based on the decision
**Continue** when:
- an important conclusion still depends on missing data;
- two credible sources contradict each other;
- a signal could overturn the leading narrative;
- a material market or segment remains uncovered;
- the available proof does not match the exact claim.

**Stop** when:
- several credible sources converge;
- more research would add precision without changing the decision;
- the question concerns a detail meant for the appendix;
- the information is not publicly available and that limit is established;
- the marginal cost of research exceeds its potential impact on the reasoning.

A new cycle starts only when a high-impact question remains open. The loop is bounded: a coverage cycle, then at most one discriminant cycle. Beyond, residual uncertainty is stated as a limit of the deck, never researched further.

## Falsification test before the storyline
Before the leading narrative becomes the storyline:
1. What evidence contradicts it?
2. What alternative explanation fits the same facts?
3. Which conclusion relies on an absence of evidence?
4. Which conclusion confuses market need with willingness to buy?
5. Which conclusion extrapolates from one country or one actor?
6. What finding would materially reverse the recommendation?
7. Has that finding been actively searched for?

## Example of the expected behaviour
After a first cycle on a European KYC platform:
- Initial expectation: country attractiveness may be the primary segmentation lens.
- Research result: country structures matter, but operating-model differences appear equally or more important.
- Updated reasoning: segment by banking model and mutualisation potential, with geography as a secondary dimension.
- New research: procurement structure by banking model, shared IT and KYC capabilities in mutualist networks, cross-country standardisation needs of multinational banks, evidence of adoption or rejection of shared KYC infrastructure.

After discovering regulatory limits:
- Previous narrative: a common platform can mutualise the KYC process across banks.
- New evidence: banks retain accountability and certain decisions cannot be delegated.
- Revised narrative: the opportunity is a configurable platform that mutualises evidence collection, orchestration and selected controls while banks keep their decisions.
- Design implication: the deck must separate platform tasks, bank-owned decisions and shared governance.

## The division of labour that follows
- The **engagement manager** decides what can reasonably be concluded and in which order the reader meets it.
- The **researchers** find and trace facts; the **fact-checker** confirms the displayed ones once.
- The **challenger**, who did not build the reasoning, tests it at gate 1.
